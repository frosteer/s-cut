FAQ

Questions, answered

Is S-Cut right for me?

Yes, if:

  • You're a solo creator or personal brand
  • Your content is mostly talking-head style
  • You want a clean, minimalist and professional look
  • You'd rather spend your time on your business than on editing

No, if:

  • You want heavy motion graphics or flashy animation
Do I need to tell you which part to use?

Not for short recordings. Just send them and we'll edit. With the Long Video Pack, we watch the footage and pick the strongest moments for you.

How long will my Reel be?

Most Reels run under 60 seconds. We make clips for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, and the final length depends on what makes the clip work best.

Do you add B-roll?

Yes, if it genuinely improves the video. We use copyright-free B-roll at no extra charge.

Do you add fancy animations?

Our standard edit is clean, minimalist and professional. We mostly use subtle fade animations at carefully adjusted speeds. Trust us: you don't need flashy animation to grow with short-form content.

Do you add motion graphics?

No. We don't create motion graphics from scratch. If you want graphics flying in from the left and right, S-Cut isn't the right fit. We focus on minimalist, clean and professional short-form content.

Can I send footage from Google Drive?

Yes. Send a Drive link or any shared folder where your footage lives.

Do you use AI?

No. Every video is 100% human edited and 100% human reviewed. A real person makes every decision, from the first cut to the final check.

Can you guarantee my Reel will go viral?

No, and nobody honestly can. What we do guarantee is a professional edit, human review, and a video that's ready to post.

What if I have over 60 minutes of footage?

The Long Video Pack covers up to 60 minutes. For longer recordings, contact us and we'll find the best option.

Ready to stop editing?

You've got the ideas and the expertise. We'll turn your recordings into content you're proud to post.
